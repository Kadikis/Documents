About Tomayhem

Tomayhem is a fast, messy first-person tomato fight. Jump into a short round against bots, grab tomatoes and splat everyone in sight before the timer runs out. No account needed, and it plays offline.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I throw?

Tap to lob a tomato, or hold to charge a longer, harder throw. A dotted arc shows where it will land.

I ran out of tomatoes.

Grab more from a tomato plant or crate in the arena. In Fiesta Plaza the tomato truck also drops fresh piles.

How do I unlock more worlds?

Every finished round earns XP for finishing, for each splat and for your place. Greenhouse Farm unlocks at level 5 and Night Market at level 10.

Aiming feels too fast or too slow.

Open Settings to change Touch sensitivity, turn Gyro aim on or off, and adjust Aim assist. Bot difficulty and Round length are there too.

The game runs slowly or my phone gets warm.

Lower the Graphics setting in Settings.

How do I start over?

Use "Reset stats & level" on the Stats screen. Deleting the app also removes all game data.

Will I lose my progress if I change phones?

Progress is saved only on your device, so it does not carry over to a new phone.

Privacy

Tomayhem has no accounts, ads, in-app purchases or analytics, and your progress stays on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.
