Tomayhem Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026-11-25

Last Updated: 2026-11-25

We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Tomayhem game ("the Application") uses, where it is stored, and who it is shared with.

Information Collection and Use

Tomayhem does not ask you for any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no online multiplayer, no advertising and no in-app purchases.

Your game progress (level, XP and round statistics) and your settings (controls, sensitivity, sound, graphics quality and similar options) are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

If you turn on gyro aiming, the Application reads your device's motion sensors to move the camera. This data is used on your device while you play and is never stored or transmitted.

The Application does not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos.

Automatically Collected Information

Tomayhem is built with the Unity game engine. The engine may automatically send limited technical information to Unity Technologies to help diagnose crashes and performance problems, such as:

  • Device information (model, operating system version, hardware specifications)
  • Crash reports and engine error information
  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address), as part of the network request

This information is not used to identify you, and we do not combine it with any other data.

Third Party Access

The third-party services below may process the information described above under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity Technologies
  • Apple App Store and Google Play, when you download or update the Application

The Service Provider may disclose Automatically Collected Information:

  • as required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena, or similar legal process;
  • when they believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect their rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request.

Deleting Your Data

You can reset your level, XP and statistics at any time from the Stats screen in the game. Uninstalling the Application removes all game data stored on your device and stops all collection of information by the Application.

CCPA and GDPR Compliance

  • The Application does not collect or store any personal information on our servers.
  • User Rights: Because your game data stays on your device, you control it directly and can delete it as described above. For information processed by Unity, Apple or Google, see their privacy policies linked above.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.
  • If you have any questions regarding privacy, contact us at karlispaegle@gmail.com.

Data Retention Policy

We do not keep any data about you. Game data stays on your device until you reset it or uninstall the Application. Technical information processed by third-party services is retained in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

Children

The Application does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13, and contains no advertising, purchases or chat. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at karlispaegle@gmail.com and we will take the necessary action.

Security

Game data is stored locally using your device's standard app storage, which is protected by your device's own security. We do not operate servers that hold your data.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically. Your continued use of the Application after any updates constitutes your acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.

Your Consent

By using the Application, you are consenting to the processing of your information as set forth in this Privacy Policy now and as amended by us.
